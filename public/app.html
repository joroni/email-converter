How to get the image links from Pardot

  1. Upload the images to Pardot Files

    In step 2, click Download images (zip) and unzip it. In Pardot go to Marketing > Content > Files, click Upload Files and choose every image from the zip. Wait until each one shows "was successfully uploaded."

  2. Copy the file list from the browser Inspector

    On that same page, right-click anywhere and choose Inspect (or press F12, or Cmd+Option+I on Mac). In the Elements tab find the line <div class="accordion" id="file-upload-accordion"> and click it so it is highlighted. Right-click it and choose Copy (in Chrome: Copy > Copy outerHTML).

  3. Paste it here and click Finalise

    Close this guide, paste into the box, and click Finalise. The tool matches each image by its original file name and fills in the Pardot links.
